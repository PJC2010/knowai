## Decision and verification boundaries

Recommended `access_status` is separate from `enabled`:

- `approved`: a future, documented source-specific decision; **none granted here**.
- `review_required`: technical metadata may be available, but applicable terms, rights or scope remain unresolved; no collection/import authorization.
- `blocked`: an observed directive or relevant publisher restriction prevents proceeding under the current evidence; requires resolution before changing status.

The parent is implementing a **DB-enforced access review independent of the enable toggle**. These recommendations are inputs to that work, not a claim that enforcement is already deployed or verified. Neither enabling a source nor explicit URL import should bypass an unresolved/blocked review.

**Live coverage:** five feeds returned parsed RSS, Anthropic's listing returned HTML, and two representative articles were checked. Four feeds and eight article probes were withheld. This is honest partial live verification, not ten accepted production integrations. **The Decoder has a definite matching feed-path disallow; Anthropic has unresolved terms applicability to its newsroom, not a proven `/news` prohibition.** Anthropic remains required scope: resolve the issue or obtain an explicit scope decision; do not silently omit it or substitute an unofficial feed.

### Method

- Plain unauthenticated HTTPS, `KnowAI-SourceReview/0.1 (read-only endpoint review)`, bounded transient reads/timeouts. No credentials, cookies, installation, paid/model calls, browser rendering, proxies, challenges/paywall bypasses, database operations or source enablement.
- Robots preceded discovery/article requests. The first feed/listing batch preceded detailed terms review; success in that batch is **not permission to repeat collection** under restrictions subsequently found. Representative articles were withheld after relevant automated-access restrictions or unresolved restriction scope appeared.
- Only metadata, response facts, minimal layout markers, policy links and brief policy findings remain here—no article bodies, full feeds, JSON dumps, footer lists or captures. `pubDate` counts show field presence, not application-parser validity. HTTP `Last-Modified` is not an article publication date.
- **Pitfall caught before requesting The Decoder feed:** Python's standard `RobotFileParser` failed to recognize its wildcard disallow. Manual review caught `*/feed/`. Use correct wildcard/group interpretation and never change user agent to evade a restriction.
- Earlier observations from the October 12 source-expansion plan are labelled historical where used. No current result is inferred from them.
